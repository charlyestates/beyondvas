Questions agents ask us

Everything you are wondering, answered.

Will my clients notice an accent?

Our VAs are screened on video for clear, confident English, and many come from US-facing call centers. Your clients hear a professional. In Spanish, they hear a native speaker, which wins you every Spanish-speaking lead in your market.

What time zone are they in?

Honduras runs on Central Standard Time all year. Your VA works your business hours, whether you are on Pacific, Mountain, Central or Eastern time.

How do I know they are actually working?

Your VA clocks in and out, their screen is captured about every two minutes, activity is scored, and you get your own access to the hours and screenshots. Idle time pauses the clock. You pay for tracked hours only.

I cannot afford it right now.

A BeyondVA is $8 to $12 an hour. Twenty hours a week at $10 is about $866 a month, with no payroll taxes, benefits or desk on top. Add up the hours you spent last week on CRM entry, listing input, scheduling and follow-up, then ask what those hours would have been worth on appointments. For most agents the math is not close.

Is my client data safe?

Every VA signs a confidentiality agreement. Access is granted through a password vault, so passwords are never shared in text, and we can revoke access instantly. VAs work inside your systems and do not download client data. You own every account.

Can an unlicensed assistant make calls for me?

Yes, within your state's rules for unlicensed assistants. Everywhere, your VA can answer calls, confirm appointments, follow up with your existing leads and clients, and do admin. Outbound prospecting is allowed in some states (California permits it under broker supervision) and restricted in others (Texas does not let an unlicensed assistant call to gauge interest in buying or selling). Your VA never negotiates, quotes terms or acts as an agent. Calls use your lists and your phone system, so Do-Not-Call and consent rules apply exactly as they do when you dial, and we train every ISA on them and set up the compliance checklist with you on day one. Confirm specifics with your broker.

Will my clients know I use a VA?

Only if you want them to. Your VA works from your email domain and your phone system and introduces themselves as part of your team, because they are. What clients notice is that someone answers, and that Spanish-speaking clients get called back in Spanish.

What if my VA quits?

We replace them free within nine months, and the new VA trains on your SOPs before day one. Your dashboard, logins and process documents stay in place.

What if it is not a good fit?

That is what the 90-day guarantee is for. Tell your BeyondVAs manager, we coach first, and if it is still not right we present replacement candidates within ten business days at no new setup fee.

I tried a VA before and it did not work.

Almost every failed VA story has the same three gaps: no training, no tracking, no manager. BeyondVAs provides all three, which is why the hourly rate includes them.

I do not have time to train someone.

You will not need to. Your VA arrives trained on real estate vocabulary, phone scripts and the tools you use. In week one they document your processes themselves, and our manager runs the kickoff and the check-ins.

I am not big enough for an assistant yet.

Nobody feels big enough until after they hire help. Every team leader you follow hired their first assistant before it felt comfortable, and that hire freed the hours they used to prospect and list. Start at 20 hours a week and grow the schedule as the business grows.

Do I have to commit to 40 hours?

The minimum is 20 hours per week. Most clients run 40 because a full-time VA learns your business faster and stays longer.

What do I need to provide?

Seats in the tools you already use: your CRM, your phone system or dialer, and an email address. We handle recruiting, training, tracking, management and payment.

What does the $499 cover?

Recruiting and vetting, onboarding training, the tracking account, your dashboard login, billing setup, and the kickoff with a BeyondVAs manager. It is charged once per VA.

Why Honduras instead of the Philippines?

Same time zone as you, native Spanish for your bilingual market, strong US call-center culture, and no twelve-hour offset. Your VA is awake when your clients are.

How fast can I start?

Interviews within three to five business days, and your VA is clocked in on your systems in about two weeks.

I am not in real estate. Can I still hire?

Yes. We started with real estate because that is where we come from, and the same VAs run phones, admin, marketing and customer care for any business.
