Work with US real estate teams. Get paid in dollars. Be treated like a professional.

LockedIn VAs places bilingual professionals from Honduras and across Latin America with real estate teams and businesses in the United States. We train you, place you, and pay you on time, every time.

  • From home100% remote from your home office, in Honduras or anywhere in Latin America, on US daytime hours. No rotating shifts, no night shifts, no commute.
  • USD payPaid in US dollars twice a month through LockedIn VAs. No chasing clients for money. We tell you the exact pay range for the role on the first call, before any test.
  • Steady hoursMost placements are 40 hours a week on US business hours with one client; some start at 20 to 30 hours. You are paid for every tracked hour.
  • TrainingA one-to-two-week onboarding academy before your first placement and a monthly training session for as long as you work with us, both at no cost to you.
  • Someone on your sideA LockedIn VAs coordinator who handles the client relationship, backs you up and coaches you.
  • GrowthA pay review at 90 days, appointment bonuses for ISAs, and a path to team lead and trainer roles.
  • RespectOur clients are real estate professionals who know your work is what grows their business.
How it works: you work with LockedIn VAs as an independent contractor, not as an employee. We pay you in US dollars, we do not withhold taxes, and you are responsible for your own taxes in your country (in Honduras most VAs invoice with an RTN). We walk you through every document before you sign.

What you need

  • Fluent English (B2 or better) and native Spanish, spoken and written
  • Internet of at least 25 Mbps down and 5 Mbps up, plus a backup connection on another carrier
  • A private, quiet workspace and a noise-cancelling headset
  • A computer with 8 GB of RAM or more, a webcam, Windows 10/11 or macOS
  • A laptop with hours of battery or a UPS, plus backup power for your modem, for outages
  • Availability for your client's US business hours (Pacific, Mountain, Central or Eastern); we tell you the exact schedule in your local time before you accept a placement
  • Comfortable working with clock-in time tracking and randomized screenshots while on the clock
  • 18 or older with a valid government ID; a criminal record certificate (antecedentes penales, or your country's equivalent) is requested only at the final stage, before placement
  • Customer service, call-center or sales experience is a plus

Before you apply.
Antes de aplicar.

    

      
Does it cost anything to apply or train?
No. Applying is free and the training is free. We never ask applicants for money. If anyone asks you to pay in our name, it is not us.

      
How and when do I get paid?
In US dollars, twice a month, through LockedIn VAs. We tell you the exact pay range for the role on the first call, before any test.

      
Do I need real estate experience?
No. Call-center, customer service or sales experience helps, and we teach you the real estate side in our training program before your first placement.

      
What hours would I work?
Your client’s US business hours, Monday to Friday in most placements. We tell you the exact schedule in your local time before you accept.

      
Am I an employee?
No. You work with LockedIn VAs as an independent contractor. We pay you in US dollars, we do not withhold taxes, and you handle your own taxes in your country.

      
How is my time tracked?
You clock in and out, and a tracker takes random screenshots only while you are on the clock. You are paid for every tracked hour.

      
What happens after I apply?
Our recruiting team reviews your application and writes to you on WhatsApp within three business days. Please apply only once.

    

    

      
¿Cuesta algo aplicar o capacitarse?
No. Aplicar es gratis y la capacitación es gratis. Nunca pedimos dinero a quienes aplican. Si alguien te pide un pago en nuestro nombre, no somos nosotros.

      
¿Cómo y cuándo me pagan?
En dólares, dos veces al mes, a través de LockedIn VAs. Te decimos el rango exacto de pago del rol en la primera llamada, antes de cualquier prueba.

      
¿Necesito experiencia en bienes raíces?
No. Ayuda tener experiencia en call center, servicio al cliente o ventas, y nosotros te enseñamos la parte de bienes raíces en nuestro programa de capacitación antes de tu primera colocación.

      
¿En qué horario trabajaría?
En el horario laboral de tu cliente en Estados Unidos, de lunes a viernes en la mayoría de las colocaciones. Te decimos el horario exacto en tu hora local antes de aceptar.

      
¿Soy empleado?
No. Trabajas con LockedIn VAs como contratista independiente. Te pagamos en dólares, no retenemos impuestos y tú te encargas de tus impuestos en tu país.

      
¿Cómo se registra mi tiempo?
Marcas entrada y salida, y un programa toma capturas de pantalla al azar solo mientras estás en horario. Se te paga cada hora registrada.

      
¿Qué pasa después de aplicar?
Nuestro equipo de reclutamiento revisa tu aplicación y te escribe por WhatsApp en tres días hábiles. Por favor aplica una sola vez.
